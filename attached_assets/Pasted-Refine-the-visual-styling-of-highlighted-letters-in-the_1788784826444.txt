Refine the visual styling of highlighted letters in the gematria app.

IMPORTANT:
The problem is NOT the card padding.
The problem is the highlighted gold background around selected letters.

Current issue:
The highlighted letters in ראשי תיבות / סופי תיבות have too much horizontal padding, so they look bulky and create too much spacing on the x-axis.

Goal:
Make the highlight background hug the selected letter tightly and elegantly, while preserving readability and the premium look.

Apply this everywhere the same highlight style is used, especially:
- Tanach result cards
- sidebar saved-match cards
- ראשי תיבות results
- סופי תיבות results

Design requirements:
- highlight only the actual selected letter(s), not the full word
- keep the gold background
- reduce horizontal padding significantly
- reduce vertical padding slightly
- keep a small subtle border radius
- do not add large margins between highlighted letters
- do not let the highlight create excessive spacing between adjacent letters
- maintain beautiful RTL rendering
- preserve niqqud / cantillation rendering behavior

Implementation guidance:
- inspect the element/class used for highlighted letters
- remove any oversized inline padding or min-width
- use a tight inline highlight style, for example:
  - display: inline-block
  - line-height: 1
  - padding-inline: about 0.12em–0.18em
  - padding-block: about 0.02em–0.08em
  - border-radius: about 4px–6px
  - margin-inline: 0.02em–0.05em at most
  - width: auto
  - min-width: 0
- if there is nested wrapping that adds extra spacing, simplify it
- avoid chunky pill-button appearance

Important:
Do NOT redesign the cards.
Do NOT change the general layout.
This is a micro-polish task focused specifically on the highlighted background around matched letters.

Desired result:
The selected letters should look like refined, tight gold highlights that sit naturally inside the Hebrew text, without creating large horizontal gaps.
