Adjust the result hierarchy on the calculator screen.

The current implementation makes **גימטריה מלאה** dramatically larger and more visually important than the other two calculations.

That is NOT what the client wants.

For this user, these are essentially **three equal calculation results**:

1. גימטריה מלאה
2. ראשי תיבות
3. גימטריית ראשי תיבות

They should feel like three parts of the same calculation, not one primary result with two minor statistics.

## Desired layout

Keep:

**גימטריה מלאה**

as a full-width card on the first row.

Then place:

**ראשי תיבות**

and

**גימטריית ראשי תיבות**

as two equal-width cards directly underneath.

However, make all three cards have approximately the SAME visual importance.

### Specifically:

* Reduce the excessive height of the current גימטריה מלאה card.
* Remove the large unused empty space inside it.
* Use approximately the same vertical padding across all three result cards.
* Use the same or very similar result-number/font size across all three cards.
* Do not make the full-gematria number dramatically larger.
* Keep borders, radius, shadows, labels, and typography consistent.
* The full-width card may retain the subtle gold accent, but it should NOT look like a hero banner.
* The two lower cards should feel like equal members of the same calculation group.
* Keep the layout compact and balanced.

Conceptually:

גימטריה מלאה
304 · דש

────────────────────────────

ראשי תיבות              גימטריית ראשי תיבות
ש                         300 · ש

The exact RTL ordering should follow the current Hebrew layout.

## Save button

Keep the שמור / כבר שמור control associated with the calculation, but do not make the full-gematria card unnecessarily tall just to accommodate it.

If necessary, place the Save control neatly immediately below the three-result group or in a compact position inside the full-width card.

## Important

Do NOT change:

* calculation logic
* matching logic
* database behavior
* word breakdown
* colors/palette
* overall design language

This is only a visual hierarchy/layout correction.

The goal is:

**three equally important calculations presented in a clean 1 + 2 card arrangement.**
