The redesigned שמורים page is close, but there are still several layout problems.

Preserve the approved visual language and fix only the structural issues described below.

## CURRENT PROBLEMS

### 1. Collapsed cards are too tall

The cards still contain too much empty vertical space relative to the amount of information shown.

They should feel compact and easy to scan.

### 2. Expanding one card breaks the two-column grid

Currently, opening one saved entry makes that one grid card much taller while the neighboring card remains short.

This creates a large awkward empty area and destroys the visual rhythm of the library.

### 3. Group-by-gematria creates giant wrappers even for unique values

When `קבץ לפי גימטריה` is enabled, even a gematria value with only ONE saved entry receives a large full-width beige group container.

This creates enormous dead space and defeats the purpose of the compact library.

### 4. Toolbar still feels slightly split

The stats on the right and controls on the left are correct conceptually, but spacing should be tightened so they read as one intentional toolbar.
